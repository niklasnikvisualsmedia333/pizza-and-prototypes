## Verification and treatment

- Jarvis was identified from existing TMP project context before checking its
  official website. It is the company led by Jan Carlo Niklas in Wenden.
- Klietsch's official imprint names Ingenieurgemeinschaft Klietsch GmbH and
  Sebastian Klietsch. No unrelated company with a similar name is used.
- AutomatIQ's official website identifies Niklas Wölfl as a co-founder and uses
  the selected logo in its header and organization metadata.
- No suitable new logo files were found in the repository or accessible Drive
  image search. All three new files come directly from official company sites.
- Klietsch's SVG geometry and colors are unchanged; trailing whitespace was
  removed. The other two logos were resized proportionally
  to 640 pixels wide and encoded as lossless WebP with transparency preserved.
  No colors, lettering or shapes were changed. Jarvis's light mark uses a dark
  surface for contrast; the other marks use a light surface.
- Logos are served locally. Only a deliberate link click opens an official
  company site; no remote logo requests or embedded media are introduced.
- The previous shared Entrepreneurship Center URL (`/startup/`) returned HTTP
  404. Its shared supporter/network link now uses the verified official `/ec`
  page, which returns HTTP 200.

## Usage review

The logos remain the property of their respective owners. The first-party
sources establish authenticity, but do not provide an explicit third-party
redistribution license. Confirm permission for the new logo placements with
the organizations during release review. The existing supporter placements and
their "Supported by" labels are retained.
